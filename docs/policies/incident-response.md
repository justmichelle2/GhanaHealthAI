# Incident Response Policy (draft for review)

**Status:** no on-call team, contact, monitoring service or approved response timeline is configured.

Before launch, name an incident lead and backup, security/privacy contacts, clinical escalation contact, and the person authorized to communicate with users and authorities. Establish a 24/7 reporting channel, severity criteria, evidence preservation, containment, credential/token rotation, backup recovery, incident log, and post-incident review. Define notification thresholds and timelines with Ghana-qualified counsel and the Data Protection Commission; this draft does not assert a specific legal deadline.

For suspected exposure of health data: restrict access and preserve relevant logs; avoid copying health content into tickets or chat; revoke affected sessions and share links; rotate potentially exposed credentials; assess scope with the data controller and counsel; notify affected parties/authorities as legally required; document decisions and remediation; and verify recovery before reopening affected features. Exercise the plan before production and periodically afterward.
