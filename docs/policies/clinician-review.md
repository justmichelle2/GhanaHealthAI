# Clinician Review Policy (draft for review)

Patient messages can be queued for review by accounts whose clinician profile an administrator has marked verified. Verification currently means an administrator changes an application flag; the software does not independently verify professional registration or credentials. Clinicians may mark a conversation reviewed and add an internal note; notes are not returned to the patient as a clinical response. AI-generated replies may appear in a conversation, but they are not clinician responses. The service does not guarantee review times.

Before launch, define credential checks, authorized scope, confidentiality, patient communication, escalation and emergency handling, review coverage, note standards, professional responsibility, audit review, complaints, adverse-event reporting and service-hour expectations. Obtain Ghana-qualified clinical and legal review. Do not imply a clinician has responded when a status is merely marked reviewed.
