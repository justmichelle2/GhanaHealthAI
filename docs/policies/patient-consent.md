# Patient Consent Policy (draft for review)

**Approval:** not approved. This draft describes the current interface and must be reviewed with Ghana-qualified privacy and clinical counsel.

Before a patient submits a message, the interface requires affirmative consent to store it for clinician review and warns users not to include emergency details. Consent is attached to the conversation submission. The current consent text does not establish a complete lawful-basis assessment or explain all provider, transfer, retention, and patient-rights details.

Conversation sharing is a separate optional action. It is disabled unless the patient opens the share dialog, sees the warning that anyone possessing the link can view the content, and checks an explicit confirmation. The server supports read-only links expiring after 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days; the owner can revoke them. Do not infer share consent from general account registration or message-storage consent.

Before launch, obtain review of age/guardian handling, consent capacity and language, withdrawal and deletion effects, clinician access, special-category health-data conditions, and how consent and notices will be recorded and versioned. Provide accurately translated consent language only after competent-speaker and clinical/legal review.
