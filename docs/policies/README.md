## Ghana review items

The Data Protection Commission says controllers and processors intending to process personal data must register; counsel and the proposed data controller must determine the project's classification, registration and supervision duties, lawful basis, data-subject rights workflow, cross-border transfer safeguards, and any impact-assessment requirements before launch. See the [DPC organisational guidance](https://dpc.gov.gh/for-organisations/) and [Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843)](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2012/843/eng%402012-05-18). Health data is sensitive personal data under Act 843; have Ghana-qualified counsel review the specific processing and consent design.

Determine with Ghana-qualified counsel and the relevant authorities whether this service's clinician-review model or future clinical functionality triggers any healthcare facility, professional-practice, medical-device, telemedicine, or other licensing obligations. HeFRA publishes licensing information for health facilities under Act 829; applicability to this software/service has not been determined. See [HeFRA](https://hefra.gov.gh/licensing-directorate/).
