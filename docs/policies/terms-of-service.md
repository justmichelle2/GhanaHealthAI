# Terms of Service (draft for review)

**Effective date:** not set · **Operator/contact:** not designated · **Approval:** not approved

Ghana Health Assistant is an early health-information and clinician-review application. AI replies provide general information, may be inaccurate, are not clinically validated, and are not a substitute for assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. Each message requires consent before it is sent to Dahl Inference, an external AI provider. Users should seek qualified care and contact local emergency services for urgent situations.

Patient accounts may submit messages for storage and clinician review. Users should avoid including information that is not needed. Sharing is off by default; creating a link requires a separate confirmation. Anyone with a created link can read the shared conversation until it expires or is revoked. Users should revoke links when no longer needed and should not share links through public channels.

The operator, availability commitments, fees, complaint channels, service boundaries, liability allocation, dispute process, governing law, age eligibility, and account suspension/deletion rules are not yet defined. They must be completed and reviewed by Ghana-qualified counsel before these terms are shown as binding. Nothing in this draft asserts regulatory approval, professional licensure, or certification.
