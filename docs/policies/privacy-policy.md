# Privacy Policy (draft for review)

**Effective date:** not set · **Controller/contact:** not designated · **Approval:** not approved

Ghana Health Assistant accepts patient accounts and stores profile fields (email, optional display name, preferred language), conversation text and AI replies, conversation language, consent timestamp, clinician-review status/notes, and security audit events. With explicit consent, the current message is sent to Dahl Inference to generate a reply; previous conversation messages are not sent. AI replies are general information, may be inaccurate, and are not clinician-reviewed or clinically validated.

The application uses a short-lived authentication cookie and a separate CSRF cookie. Passwords are hashed before storage. Recovery emails and share links become available only when SMTP is configured and the owner explicitly confirms a share link. A share link allows anyone who obtains it to read that conversation until expiry (maximum seven days) or revocation. Public views omit account IDs and profile details, but the conversation itself may identify the patient through its content.

The application does not currently integrate analytics, advertising, file uploads, or voice recording. Dahl Inference is an external AI provider; its data handling, hosting locations, retention, and applicable processor terms must be verified and disclosed before use with real patients. Hosting, email, database, backup and monitoring providers have not been selected; their data access and locations must also be disclosed before launch.

No account self-service deletion flow or automatic conversation retention period is currently implemented. A patient can delete an individual conversation in the application; audit records and external backups may persist. The controller must publish validated contact details and processes for access, correction, objection, export, deletion, complaints and other applicable rights.

This is a product description, not a legal policy. Ghana-qualified counsel must verify lawful basis, sensitive health data conditions, consent language, controller/processor roles, DPC registration/supervision, international transfers, retention and rights handling before use with real patients.
